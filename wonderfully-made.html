Our Story

Cheyenne was born with Short Gut Syndrome.

Short Gut Syndrome, also called Short Bowel Syndrome, is a rare condition in which a child’s body does not have enough functioning small intestine to absorb the nutrients it needs.

For many families, that can mean feeding tubes, IV nutrition, surgeries, and long stretches inside hospital walls.

That was our story.

Before she was six months old, we had already faced more than most families do in a lifetime.

We learned medical terms we never wanted to know. We counted ounces. We watched monitors. We prayed through the night.

And we watched God carry us.

This book is not simply a marketing example. It was written in honor of my daughter’s strength and as a testimony to God’s goodness during our hardest season.

Wonderfully Made was born from that journey so that other parents walking hospital halls can place a story in their child’s hands that speaks courage, identity, hope, and truth.

No diagnosis defines a child.

Every child is fearfully and wonderfully made.

Dedication Example

For Cheyenne Suzette Grimes,

My brave girl.

It was the greatest honor of my life to stand beside you while you fought your way into strength.

I watched you endure more before six months old than most do in a lifetime.
I watched you learn to walk with a pole beside you.
I watched you smile through scars.

And I would do it all again in a heartbeat.

Every surgery.
Every long night.
Every prayer whispered over your hospital bed.

Because you are worth everything.

God has been so good to us.

Love,
Mom
