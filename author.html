About the Author

Sabrina C. Mansour

Author • Illustrator • Founder of Mansour Press

I never set out simply to write books.

I believe God uses stories to shape hearts, awaken wonder, and remind us of His truth. Whether through a gentle bedtime story, a work of discipleship, or a book exploring the beauty of His creation, my prayer is always the same—that every page points beyond itself and toward the One who wrote the greatest story of all.

Mansour Press was founded from a desire to create books that are beautifully crafted, biblically grounded, and worthy of being treasured for generations. While our journey begins with children's literature, the vision reaches far beyond a single genre. My hope is to publish works that inspire faith, cultivate wisdom, encourage stewardship of God's creation, and help readers of every age know Christ more deeply.

As both an author and illustrator, I approach every project with the conviction that excellence is an act of worship. Every story, every brushstroke, and every word is prayerfully created with the hope of reflecting God's beauty, His truth, and His enduring love.

These books are more than stories to me. They are an offering. My prayer is that parents, children, and readers of every age would close each book with a deeper appreciation for God's character, His creation, and the hope found in Christ.

If someone finishes one of my books remembering only my name, then I have missed the point. But if they finish it seeing Christ more clearly, marveling at His creation, and longing to know Him more deeply, then the work has accomplished its purpose.

To God be all the glory.
